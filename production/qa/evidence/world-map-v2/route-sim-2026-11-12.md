# World Map v2 — route simulation (Hellfire floor 2) and paired full runs, 2026-11-12

Tool: `tools/balance/deck_sim.gd` (deterministic since Phase 0; `--route-policy` added in Phase 7/9; the probe bot
keeps its lane past the hub — the game lets the player switch there). Bot: dodge 0.85 (TD-006, unanchored), static
shop / relic policies (TD-037). Every number is a bot number: a relative yardstick, never an absolute difficulty claim.

## Commands

Probe (one process per character, the 7 policies in sequence; `--floors=2..2` = hellfire only, started with the
starter deck at full HP):

```
GODOT=C:/Users/ADMIN/AppData/Local/Microsoft/WinGet/Links/godot.exe
$GODOT --headless --path . -s res://tools/balance/deck_sim.gd -- --run=v6 --region-pick=order --seeds=70 --seed-offset=2001 \
  --characters=<char> --policies=build --shop-policy=greedy --event-policy=risky --relic-policy=pick --tables=v6 \
  --floors=2..2 --final=off --route-policy=<safe|treasure|peril|elite|mystery|random|value>
```

Paired full run (same 70 seeds and flags as the v1 baseline, plus `--route-policy=value`):

```
$GODOT --headless --path . -s res://tools/balance/deck_sim.gd -- --run=v6 --region-pick=order --seeds=70 --seed-offset=2001 \
  --characters=<char> --policies=build --shop-policy=greedy --event-policy=risky --relic-policy=pick --tables=v6 --final=on --route-policy=value
```

Raw: `route-sim-hellfire-f2-<policy>-<char>.txt` (35 files), `fullrun-v2hellfire-value-<char>.txt` (5), log
`route-sim-runlog.txt` (the first probe pass, before the lane-keeping fix, was overwritten; the log keeps both passes'
timings).

## 1. Lane probe — per archetype (policy == the lane taken; 5 characters pooled, weighted by runs)

(the generator draws 3 of 5 archetypes per map: safe 190, treasure 245, peril 235, elite 205 of 350 visits; the
`mystery` lane never appears on hellfire floor 2 for these seeds — its weight 0.8 against hellfire's peril ×1.5 and
the safe/treasure guarantee leave it out of the three draws in this seed set; see the limitation below).

\* NV = gold + 60 × relics gained (mean shop relic price) − 1.6 × HP lost pts (shop heal: 40 gold per 25 %); death
risk is not priced in (the probe starts at full HP each run; see region clear %).

Against the `06` §3 targets:

| Target | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Peril NV ≥ Safe + 25 % | 174.7 vs 127.1 = **+37 %** | met (bot) |
| Peril HP cost = Safe + 15–25 pts | +8.7 pts | below the band (cheaper than planned) |

| Elite: ≈ 1 relic per visit, miniboss loss ≤ 20 % | relics +0.28 vs safe; region clear 88.8 (−1.7) | relic row works; loss inside band |
| Treasure ≥ Safe + 20 % gold, +1 ink | gold **−9.5 %** (the chest pays a card / relic / ink, not drops); inks not counted by the sim | not met on gold; INFO |
| Heat 3–4 reached on peril ≥ 60 % | the probe bot does not print the gauge; the integration test shows heat 3 before the hub and 4 after the gamble on the peril lane | not measured by the sim (limitation) |

Reading: Peril pays as designed (gold, relics, luck) but the bot dies there 2.3× as often as on Safe — the vents at
heat 3–4 plus two modifiers are too much for a bot that cannot read vent warnings well. The objective (`no_hit_3`)
is met in 25 % of peril visits. Treasure's value is in the chest (not counted as drops) and the forge.

## 5. Verdict for the slice

- Routes have distinguishable consequences: Peril +18 % gold, +0.8 relics, +9 HP pts lost, −17 pts region clear;
  Elite +9 % gold, +0.3 relics; Treasure −10 % drop gold (value in the chest / forge); Safe the reference.
- The reward side meets its target; the **risk side of Peril exceeds its band for four of five heroes** (bot). This is
  the tuning the approval package asked to measure before changing numbers (D-R1 / D6): recommended first knobs, in
  `region_rules.json` / `rewards.json` only — heat 3 `vent_tick_damage` 3 → 2, heat 4 `vent_interval_sec` 4.0 → 4.5,
  or the peril slot-3 modifier count 2 → 1. Not changed in this pass (the user's "do not modify unrelated economy
  values to force simulation targets" and the one-pass budget); re-run this file's commands after any change.
- Not measured by the sim: inks, the Heat gauge distribution at the warden, the mystery lane (never drawn on
  hellfire floor 2 with these seeds), the two combat-only objectives.
